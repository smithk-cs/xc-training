Injury log

Peak load ratio is the highest weekly ratio in the two training weeks before the injury began. Click a row to see that athlete’s weekly miles above.

Load ratio is an athlete’s miles this week divided by their own average over the previous four weeks. A ratio above 1.3 means mileage jumped more than 30%. It is a screening signal, not a diagnosis.
