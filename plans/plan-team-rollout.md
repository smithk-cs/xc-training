# Plan: rolling out to the team

## Approvals

- [ ] **Athletic director and district.** Who approves student-data tools (often district IT, under a student data
      privacy agreement). Share: what is stored (runs, laps, heart rate, results, goals, injuries, pain and effort
      reports; no GPS routes or locations), who sees it (staff accounts; injury notes visible to trainer and admins
      only; each athlete sees only their own page), the AI (athletes sent as codes, never names; written notes and
      descriptions never sent; review Anthropic's commercial terms), and deletion (admin deletes an athlete and all
      their data; disconnecting revokes Garmin access).
- [ ] **Communications office.** Permission form for the school name and logo in the app; request the approved logo
      file and exact red and gold values.
- [ ] **Claude API account owner** agreed, with a monthly budget.

## Parent consent

- [ ] Send the consent form (wording in `TEAM_SETUP.md`, section 2); keep signed copies
- [ ] Athletes also confirm parent approval on their link page before connecting

## Before connect night

- [ ] Roster imported (`name, gender, grade, group`)
- [ ] Staff accounts: head coach (admin), assistants (coach), athletic trainer (trainer)
- [ ] Last season's results and this season's goals imported; training groups and targets checked on Groups
- [ ] Download every athlete's link (Athletes page); mail-merge one link per athlete to athlete and parent
- [ ] Remind athletes to bring phones with Garmin Connect installed and signed in

## Connect night (15 minutes at practice)

- [ ] Athletes open their link, tick parent approval, tap Connect Garmin, sign in, allow activities
- [ ] Watch the Athletes page fill with "Connected"; note anyone stuck
- [ ] Show athletes My runs: effort after each run, and "Tell coaches why" on days without one
- [ ] Non-Garmin athletes: show the .fit upload on their page

## First two weeks

- [ ] Chase athletes not connected or not syncing
- [ ] Remind athletes to rate effort after workouts and long runs at minimum
- [ ] Trainer reviews athlete injury reports on Injuries and adds real injuries to the log
- [ ] Staff read the watch list and AI briefing each morning; collect feedback

## Communication

| Who | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| Parents | Consent form and what is collected | 2 weeks before connect night |
| Athletes | Personal link and how to connect | 1 week before |
| Athletes | Effort, pain and missed-day reporting | Connect night, then weekly reminder |
| Staff | Daily routine and roles | Before the pilot |
| AD / district | Pilot results and any issues | End of pilot |
