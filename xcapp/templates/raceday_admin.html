Race Day

A live race clock, tap screens for coaches along the course, team scores at each spot, a scoreboard for the livestream, and a watch page for families.

How it works
  1. Make a board for the meet below, then open Coach tap screen while signed in. Under Setup add the teams with their jersey colors and the spots where coaches will stand (1 Mile, 2 Mile, Finish).
  2. Pin each spot (optional). On a course walk, stand at each marker and tap Pin this spot here, or paste a Google Maps link. On race day each phone checks it is at its spot.
  3. Send the tap link to the coaches at the course. They don't need an account: they pick their spot and tap a jersey color each time a runner passes.
  4. At the gun, a signed-in coach presses START. Every phone's clock runs from that moment.
  5. Video: any tap screen can record with the race clock on the picture, then save the video for YouTube.
  6. Families get the watch link: the clock, the scores, and the team's YouTube livestream if you add its link in Setup. They can't change anything.

Anyone with a link can open it, so share each one only with the people it's for. New links stops the old ones working.
