How times are adjusted

The adjusted pace is the race pace, slowed by the day’s conditions (a percentage), minus what the course adds (seconds per mile).

  • Temperature (Daniels, table 6.2): none at {{ CS.TEMP_BASE_F }}°F or cooler, then about {{ CS.TEMP_PCT_PER_F }}% slower per °F. Built on marathons by fast runners, so a starting point for high school 5Ks.
  • Altitude (Daniels, table 6.1, acclimatized runners): little below 1,000 ft; about {{ CS.ALTITUDE[1][1] }}% at 1,000 m (3,281 ft), {{ CS.ALTITUDE[2][1] }}% at 1,500 m, {{ CS.ALTITUDE[3][1] }}% at 2,000 m.
