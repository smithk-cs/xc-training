For runs that didn’t sync, or a watch that isn’t Garmin. In Garmin Connect on a computer, open a run and choose Export Original. For a quick history, Garmin Connect’s Activities list has Export CSV; that file adds each run’s date, miles, time and heart rate (but not laps).
