Green 90% or more, amber 80 to 89%, red below 80%. Each day is judged on its own: miles against the day’s planned workout miles (on other days, the weekly miles goal ÷ 6 running days), and points against the day’s planned points (on other days, the weekly target ÷ 6). The Miles and Points columns are the week so far against the share due by today (a seventh of the weekly goals a day). The Score column’s color shows whether the athlete is on track for the week: the same 80% points, 20% days score, measured against what’s due so far. Tick “Show why” or point at a number to see the sums. Each group’s team workouts are on its group row.

Miles, points and heart rate come from synced runs; effort and pain from what the athlete reported. Flags are for the coach and don’t change the score: easy days run faster than easy pace, points up more than 10% on the week before, and treadmill runs whose watch pace may be off.
