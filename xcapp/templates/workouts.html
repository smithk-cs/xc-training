Workouts

Plan a workout as parts: sets × reps × a distance (meters or miles) or a time (minutes) at a pace. Each athlete’s pace for every part comes from their VDOT (easy, long run, tempo, interval and repetition, as on the VDOT page), and they see their own times on their page for the next 7 days, from their current VDOT and from their goal. A run synced that day is matched to its group’s workout automatically (the one with the most reps, then the longest), and the workout then comes off the athlete’s list. Each night the AI checks matched runs from athletes who agreed to AI analysis against the workout: done, partly or not done.
