Training groups

Each athlete goes in the highest group their best VDOT reaches. VDOT is worked out from every 1 mile, 1.5 mile, 2 mile and 5K result in the look-back window, then read against the team’s 1600 targets below: a result counts as VDOT 60 if its 1600-equivalent time reaches the VDOT 60 target. Garmin’s own watch VO2max isn’t shared through Garmin’s developer API, so results are used instead. Cross country courses vary, so coaches can pin any athlete to a group on their athlete page.
