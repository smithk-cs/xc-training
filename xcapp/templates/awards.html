How the awards are worked out

From the race results for {{ season.label }} ({{ season.start.strftime('%b %-d') }} to {{ season.end.strftime('%b %-d, %Y') }}). Classes go by each athlete’s grade that season: Freshmen are 9th grade, Sophomores 10th, Varsity 11th and 12th.

The window: the two meets picked above (by default each class’s first and last meet of the season). Those meets and every race in between count, for both awards and for who qualifies.

Who counts: athletes who ran at least {{ min_pct }}% of the meets their class raced in the window (a meet where any athlete of that class ran), so freshmen aren’t held to meets only varsity entered.

MVP: lowest average finishing place over every race in the window, since in cross country a runner scores the points of their place. Only races whose results list a place count. Ties go to more races, then the faster average adjusted time.

Most improved: every race in the window as an adjusted mile pace (the time adjusted for the course and weather, as on the Races page). A 5K is its own pace; another distance counts as the 5K pace it’s worth (by VDOT), since a 2-mile race is run faster a mile than a 5K. A best-fit line through all of an athlete’s races, over the dates, shows the trend; the improvement is how much faster the line is at their last race than at their first, so one good or bad day at either end doesn’t decide it.
