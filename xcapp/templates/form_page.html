How to film

  1. From the side, level with the runner's hips, about 10–15 m (30–50 ft) away. A track straight works well.
  2. Hold the phone still (a tripod or a fence helps) in landscape, or follow the runner smoothly.
  3. The whole body in frame, head to feet, for at least 4 steps. Good light, nobody else crossing.
  4. At the pace you want to check (easy run, tempo or race pace). Slow motion (120 or 240 fps) makes contact and flight times more accurate.

The video stays on this device: the pose model runs here and only the measurements are saved to the athlete's record.
