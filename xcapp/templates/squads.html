How team points work

Each week the squads are ranked in three ways, and the best squad gets as many team points as there are squads, the next one fewer, down to 1 (ties share):

  • Miles: the squad’s average share of each athlete’s weekly miles goal, so a newer runner who hits a smaller goal helps as much as a varsity runner who hits a bigger one. Each athlete counts up to 110%.
  • Practice points: the squad’s average share of each athlete’s weekly points target (the Week page’s points), up to 110%.
  • Races, in weeks with races: in each race, every squad runner earns one point for each runner from another squad they finish ahead of.

Athletes count for the squad they are on now, for every week, so a move carries their weeks with them.
